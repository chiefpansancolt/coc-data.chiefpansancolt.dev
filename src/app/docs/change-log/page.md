## Version [0.18.0](https://github.com/chiefpansancolt/clash-of-clans-data/releases/tag/0.18.0)

### Added

New Home Village content, including hero equipment (Revenge Deck, Monolith Arrow, Electro Fangs),
three new phase 4 crafted defenses (Hot Candle, Hero Hunter, Cake-A-Pult), and the April 2026
update's Town Hall 18 troop, spell, and defense levels.

### Changed

Build, upgrade, and research time reductions applied across defenses, traps, army buildings,
resource buildings, troops, spells, and heroes. Phase 3 crafted defenses (Roaster, Air Bombs, Lava
Launcher) are now marked as former (`isCurrent: false`).

## Version [0.17.0](https://github.com/chiefpansancolt/clash-of-clans-data/releases/tag/0.17.0)

### Added

April and June 2026 content update: the Ruin Witch troop, Angry Spell, and additional Town Hall 18
levels across multiple categories.
